## Antifragility parts guide

Each child is one validated operation. When the target is known, open only its
part-local contract, source profile, README, and validation route. Keep focused
payload under that child; do not use `parts/` as a generic storage district.

Return generated or live questions to their authored profile and stronger
owner. Presence of a part or fixture does not establish adaptation, proof, or
runtime health.
