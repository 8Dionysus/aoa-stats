# AGENTS.md

## Agon legacy guide

This directory preserves historical landing notes and closed quests after the
active Agon payload moved into parts. When historical context is the selected
question, use `INDEX.md` and `former-routes.json` before opening `raw/`.

- Treat `raw/` as historical evidence, not current route law.
- Do not add active configs, schemas, scripts, tests, or generated artifacts.
- Update the relocation ledger when a former route or active replacement
  changes.
- Preserve raw files byte-for-byte unless correcting the historical artifact is
  the explicit task.
